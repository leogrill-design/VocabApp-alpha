# VocabApp (alpha)

VocabApp is for collecting the words you meet while reading and learning them. Look a word up, save
it with where you found it, and review it until it sticks. The dictionary is built in, so it works
offline.

This is an **alpha**, for friends and testers, and **Android only** for now.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/leogrill-design/VocabApp-alpha/releases) on your
   phone and tap **VocabApp-….apk** under *Assets* to download it (about 245 MB).
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say it can't install apps from this source: tap
   **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source** for your browser or Files app, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the app, choose **Install
   anyway**: the warning appears for any app that is not from the Play Store.
4. Open VocabApp. The first launch takes a few seconds while it sets up the dictionary.

## Update

Download the new APK from the [releases page](https://github.com/leogrill-design/VocabApp-alpha/releases)
and install it over the old one, the same way. **Your words are kept.**

**Never uninstall the app to update it.** Uninstalling deletes everything you have saved. If you ever
have to, make a backup first: ☰ → **Backup** → **Export backup**.

### Automatic update notices (optional)

[Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) can tell you when a new version is out and install
it for you:

1. Install Obtainium from its [releases page](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium/releases).
2. In Obtainium, tap **Add App** and paste `https://github.com/leogrill-design/VocabApp-alpha`.
3. Turn on **Include prereleases** (every alpha is one), then tap **Add**.

## Privacy

- **The app collects nothing about you.** No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. It
  never asks for your name or email.
- **Everything stays on your phone:** your words, your statistics and your feedback. Nothing
  leaves it unless you export a backup and send or upload it yourself.
- **It never connects to the internet.** The dictionary is built in, and once installed the
  app works in airplane mode.
- Anything you type yourself, such as a note, a book title or a feedback report, goes into your
  backup as you wrote it.

## Feedback

In the app, open ☰ → **Feedback** or **Feature request**. Nothing is sent anywhere: what you write
is kept in the app. To send it, open ☰ → **About this alpha** → **Send to the developer**: your
email app opens with your backup attached, ready to send. Your backup also holds your saved words
and review history, which helps show how the app is used.
